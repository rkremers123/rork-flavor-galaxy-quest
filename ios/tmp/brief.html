Previous iteration had too much on screen at once, making the explorer too small and planets feel crowded. This brief redesigns the journey to show only 2 planets at a time with a larger explorer character as the focal point.

Goal: Focus the user's attention on the current journey segment. Increase planet size. Make explorer 3x larger and a focal point of the screen.

#2: Zig-Zag Connector Between Planets (Replace Stars)

Goal: Remove the star image connectors. Replace with a flowing, organic zig-zag or curved trail visual that connects planets vertically.

Visual Design Options

Recommended: Flowing Wave/River Connector

Style: Organic, flowing line that curves left-right (zig-zag pattern) as it flows downward between planets

  • Shape: SVG path with cubic bezier curves (not sharp angles)
  • Color: Gradient from cyan (#00d9ff) → gold (#d4af37) → pink (#e91e63)
  • Width: 3-6px stroke (mobile 3px, desktop 6px)
  • Opacity: 30-50% (ethereal, not solid)
  • Height: 120-220px (spans gap between planets)
  • Glow: Soft blur/shadow for depth (box-shadow or filter: drop-shadow)
  • Animation (Optional): Subtle flow animation (particles or shimmer flowing down the path)

SVG Path Example

State-Based Styling

Connection State Appearance Opacity Animation
Locked (Planet not yet reached) Desaturated gray 15% None
Active (User progressing toward next planet) Full gradient (cyan→gold→pink) 40% Subtle flow/shimmer (optional)
Completed (Planet unlocked) Full gradient, brighter 50% Steady glow

Explorer Animation

Dynamic Effects (Keep These)

  • Bob Animation: Gentle up-down float (5-7px offset, 0.8s loop)
  • Pulse/Strobe: Subtle opacity flicker (0.8 → 1.0, 1.5s loop)
  • Glow Effect: Soft drop-shadow around explorer for depth
